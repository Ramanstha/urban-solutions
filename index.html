About

Urban Solutions was founded on a simple but powerful purpose — to bring Meaningful Services to Nepal's urban economy. Services that are organised, reliable, and built around the people who need them. What began as a single idea in 2020 has grown into a multi-vertical platform trusted by some of Nepal's most prominent digital businesses. Here is how the story unfolded.

  • 2020

    The Beginning

    Urban Solutions was established with the launch of The Laundry Hub — Nepal's first organised laundry pickup and return service. Driven by the belief that everyday services deserved to be done properly, this was the company's first step toward building Meaningful Services for Kathmandu's urban households.

  • 2020

    Bringing Structure to E-Commerce Delivery

    Urban Solutions introduced U.S. Logistics — a last-mile delivery service built for online merchants. Beyond just moving parcels, the service was designed to solve two critical pain points: disciplined COD collection with reliable cash flow back to vendors, and clear, consistent communication at every step of the delivery process. Merchants finally had a logistics partner they could trust

  • 2021-2023

    Expanding Our Services

    Urban Solutions is redefining convenience through innovative, customer-focused services designed for modern urban living. With the launch of Instant Delivery, we entered the on-demand logistics space by offering a premium, flat-rate express delivery solution for restaurants and hyperlocal businesses — providing a faster, more reliable, and commission-free alternative to traditional aggregator platforms. Expanding our ecosystem further, The Laundry Hub became Nepal’s first organized laundry platform, connecting customers with trusted cleaning partners for seamless doorstep laundry care. From pickup to professionally cleaned delivery within 12 to 72 hours, we eliminate the hassle of queues and drop-offs, bringing convenience, efficiency, and quality service directly to your doorstep.

  • 2024

    Daraz Partnership & B2B Fleet Solutions

    A landmark partnership with Daraz Nepal gave birth to a new vertical — B2B Fleet Solutions. Urban Solutions began deploying dedicated riders within Daraz's operations, establishing Nepal's first structured subscription-based rider deployment service for enterprise clients.

  • 2025

    Gogoro Partnership

    Urban Solutions established a partnership with Gogoro, integrating electric scooters into its rider fleet. A strategic move toward an asset-light, sustainable operations model — lowering costs, improving rider retention, and reflecting the company's commitment to building services that are meaningful not just for today, but for the long term.
