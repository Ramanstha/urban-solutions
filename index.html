About

We are Kathmandu's last-mile infrastructure platform — a managed rider network powering four urban services under one roof. From doorstep delivery to laundry pickup, instant express runs to enterprise fleet solutions, Urban Solutions moves what matters, when it matters. Built in Nepal. Built for Nepal.
